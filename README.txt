StretchWay — Part A (Core App Package)

Files:
- index.html
- logo.svg
- favicon.ico, apple-touch.png, splash.png
- icon_*.png sizes (16..512)
- manifest.json
- README.txt

How to preview:
1) Unzip and open index.html in your browser.
2) Toggle theme with "Toggle Light/Dark".
3) Click categories — nav is sticky and anchors instantly to sections.

To publish:
- Use Netlify Drop: https://app.netlify.com/drop -> drag & drop the extracted folder.
